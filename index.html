        Mission field guide

        Your mission contract sets the goal. Build a design that passes the checks, then operate it without exhausting the spacecraft.

        01  Build by hand

        Tap a part or drag it onto a matching blueprint slot. Tap a fitted part to remove it. Use the Science tab to choose a second instrument. Extras occupy two optional slots.

        02  Plan the trade-offs

        Adjust propellant below the blueprint. Configure Systems opens tap cards for orbit, power, thermal protection and mission planning. Qualification tests carry benefits into flight. Cost details explain the full mission budget.

        03  Solve spacecraft problems

        Troubleshoot a power bus, inspect sensors, route around hazards, reconnect radio wiring and align thrusters. Launch keeps one short arrival-burn calculation with a workpad and calculator. Autopilot remains available with a resource penalty.

        04  Keep science flowing

        Hold BURN during orbit insertion; release after periapsis rises above 150 km. In operations, hold SCAN over the observation zone, then Downlink or D to send buffered data. Watch energy during shadow and diagnose incoming alerts.

        Hint terminal & funding

        The small top-right cheat sheet gives three 12-second consultations, then recovers for 45 active seconds. Required flight checks always remain available. Government events may interrupt your build; Practice Pause stops that clock.

        Classroom model: ideal rocket equation, heat balance, two-body gravity and Kepler timing; illustrative budgets, hardware and outcomes. Not a validated spacecraft design tool.
